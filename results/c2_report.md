Teacher-forced on the unmasked answers, S = 32. Disagreement d: masked top-1 differs from the unmasked token. Three versions of d: pooled over all answer tokens of a cell (the H13 bar), pooled over segments 0-3 only (positions both tasks reach: GSM8K answers reach up to 16 segments, summaries up to 4), and the mean of per-item d (each item weighs the same). Denominators: items and tokens of the teacher-forced run, tokens in segments 0-3 in brackets, diverged over free-run items. The EOS step is not scored in teacher-forced d: d covers the answer tokens only. A masked run that stops early or late shows in the free-run first divergence instead, where an answer that is a prefix of the other diverges at the shorter length. First divergence and recovery come from the free-running masked records against k0, over items whose answer diverged. Recovery: share still correct (GSM8K), mean ROUGE-L against the k0 answer (summarize). Generated by `c2.py report`.
